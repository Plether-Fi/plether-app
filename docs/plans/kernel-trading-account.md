# ZeroDev Kernel trading account implementation plan

**Proposal for a fresh Plether Perps deployment.** Create every Trading Account with Kernel from launch, starting on Arbitrum Sepolia. Keep the connected wallet as the root signer for the first release. Users set their gas preference with a **Gas sponsorship** toggle in the sponsored transaction modal. Both modes use the same trading account, positions, margin and self-hosted Alto bundler.

Build a Plether-operated paymaster for Kernel using the current gateway, signer, gas budget and canonical recovery designs. Use one approved Kernel account profile, one EntryPoint and one Kernel paymaster for the new deployment. First release scope covers ordinary Perps trading and account actions. Passkeys, session keys, EIP-7702, external gas sponsorship and cross-chain gas funding are later product work. [Current manifest](../../apps/frontend/public/perps-aa-manifest.json) · [Paymaster source](https://github.com/Plether-Fi/plether-core/blob/e35cf0e0e9b880e07812e36321dee98bd299dcbd/packages/perps-aa/src/PletherVerifyingPaymaster.sol) · [ZeroDev infrastructure interoperability](https://docs.zerodev.app/api-and-toolings/infrastructure/choose-an-infra-provider)

The pinned local qualification exercised first deployment and a change of payer on the same account in both directions with the patched Alto SafeMode image, a staked ZeroDev Staker and a staked Plether paymaster. The self-hosted gateway, reconciliation and deployment configuration are opt-in. Sepolia activation depends on attesting the public contract bindings and qualifying actual Nitro operations, live Arbitrum fee estimation and real wallets on an isolated canary. [Qualification harness](../../scripts/kernel-qualification/run.mjs) · [Kernel AA runbook](../runbooks/kernel-aa.md)

The frontend adds deployment-scoped account identity, the saved activity-modal preference, exact fee review and durable recovery. The local Alto harness also exercises fee-bumped cancellation for deployed and counterfactual accounts. Onboarding and public activation follow this frontend work. [Frontend and recovery runbook](../runbooks/kernel-aa.md#frontend-review-and-operation-recovery)

The onboarding flow verifies the funding address, shows ETH and EntryPoint balances, and checks the installed account at first inclusion. The pinned local harness runs both payment modes with simulated embedded and external wallet interfaces and covers withdrawal during a sponsor pause. External and Privy wallet acceptance and Nitro operations remain public rollout gates. [Onboarding runbook](../runbooks/kernel-aa.md#onboarding-and-gas-funding) · [Qualification results](../../scripts/kernel-qualification/README.md)

## Payment choice and account funding

| | Gas sponsorship on | Gas sponsorship off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who pays network gas | Plether’s paymaster, subject to eligibility, availability and budget | The Kernel account’s native ETH and available deposit at the selected EntryPoint |
| What the user signs | A Kernel UserOperation with Plether paymaster data | A Kernel UserOperation with all paymaster fields absent |
| What review shows | Plether as gas payer, with separate protocol fees and keeper charges | Estimated gas, maximum network gas in ETH, available gas funds and any shortfall, with separate protocol fees and keeper charges |
| When unavailable | Show the reason and link to the gas toggle in the sponsored transaction modal | Offer to add ETH or link to the gas toggle when sponsorship is available |

Place a single **Gas sponsorship** switch in the **Trading Account activity** modal opened from the wallet header. This is the existing sponsored transaction modal. Keep it accessible with an empty activity history and when sponsorship is paused. Default the switch to on for a new account. Store the choice on the device by chain, deployment and trading account, and apply it to future transactions across reloads and browser tabs. Show **Sponsored by Plether** when on and **Pay with ETH** when off. Transaction reviews display the selected payer and cost as read-only information. [Activity modal](../../apps/frontend/src/components/SponsoredOperationActivity.tsx#L1042) · [Wallet header](../../apps/frontend/src/components/wallet/ConnectButton.tsx#L121)

When the setting changes before signing, invalidate any open unsigned review prepared under the previous mode and refresh it before the user approves. If Plether already issued a sponsorship authorization, retain its reservation until safe reconciliation. Signed and submitted operations keep their recorded payer and nonce. Changing the switch affects future operations and leaves existing recovery intact. If sponsorship is denied or unavailable, explain why and let the user open the modal to turn sponsorship off. Prepare a new quote only after that explicit change. A sponsor error does not initiate an ETH charge.

Show **Trading Account ETH**, **EntryPoint ETH deposit** when nonzero, and **Perps margin** separately. The owner wallet can send native ETH on the correct Arbitrum network to the verified trading address, including a verified counterfactual address. The app offers the wallet transfer or a copyable funding address. The wallet pays the fee for its top-up. Verify the transfer and re-estimate the original operation. An account’s first UserOperation may include deployment gas. Confirm the factory derivation and first-operation path before showing a prefunding address. [ERC-4337 account validation and creation](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337)

Derive the maximum gas amount from the final version-specific EntryPoint prefund rules. For EntryPoint 0.9 and an operation without a paymaster, the maximum is `(verificationGasLimit + callGasLimit + preVerificationGas) × maxFeePerGas`. Preserve Arbitrum data costs in the validated bundler estimate and tune execution headroom for the user-paid path, since EntryPoint can charge for unused gas. Prove Alto can estimate an ETH shortfall for an unfunded account. If it requires a balance override, restrict that to server-controlled advisory estimation and validate the funded account against real state before final preparation and relay. Before signing, check that liquid ETH covers the part of prefund missing from the EntryPoint deposit plus any ETH call value. Refresh review if the operation or its fee bounds change. Display the actual gas charge from the operation event, including for included reverts. [EntryPoint 0.9 implementation](https://github.com/eth-infinitism/account-abstraction/blob/v0.9.0/contracts/core/EntryPoint.sol) · [Arbitrum fee model](https://docs.arbitrum.io/arbitrum-essentials/how-to-estimate-gas)

Provide owner-approved withdrawal of spare ETH and recovery of residual EntryPoint deposit to the verified owner. Account for the withdrawal operation’s own prefund and execution order. Unused prefund can remain in the EntryPoint deposit after execution. Qualify a withdrawal path that works during sponsor outages, including a safe way to handle a final small balance. Show the amount the user can withdraw before approval. [EntryPoint settlement](https://github.com/eth-infinitism/account-abstraction/blob/v0.9.0/contracts/core/EntryPoint.sol#L827)

Apply the saved setting to normal deposit, withdraw, margin top-up, open or increase, full or partial close, and configured owner protection or claim actions where applicable. Keep order commitment and keeper execution status distinct. If the new deployment offers close assistance, its keeper bounty subsidy stays within the reviewed sponsored flow. ETH-paid closes use the ordinary close and its usual collateral and bounty requirements. Sepolia welcome allocations fund settlement collateral. [Close assistance](../runbooks/sponsored-close.md) · [Testnet margin funding](../runbooks/testnet-margin-funding.md)

## 1. Qualify the account and EntryPoint

Use a factory-created Kernel v4 account with EntryPoint 0.9 as the first Sepolia candidate. Pin an exact Kernel variant and release, ECDSA validator, client library or Viem adapter, EntryPoint, Alto image and simulation contracts. Verify canonical deployed bytecode, network support, factory derivation, root validator and exact operation hash and signature behavior. Kernel v4 documents EntryPoint 0.9. The TypeScript SDK examples and current `getEntryPoint` helper are centered on older versions, so qualify an explicitly supported SDK path or a narrow Viem adapter backed by upstream artifacts. Record the supported combination before locking in release artifacts. [Kernel v4](https://github.com/zerodevapp/kernel/blob/v4.0/README.md) · [SDK EntryPoint helper](https://github.com/zerodevapp/sdk/blob/main/packages/core/constants.ts) · [Viem `toSmartAccount`](https://viem.sh/account-abstraction/accounts/smart/toSmartAccount)

Demonstrate root-signed first deployment with each gas mode, using two fresh addresses. Prefund the ETH-paid address and use a prototype Plether verifier for the sponsored address. Then switch the gas setting and perform the next operation on the same deployed account. Compare local, backend, bundler and onchain UserOperation hashes, nonce handling, fee estimates and canonical event decoding.

Qualify Alto against the selected Kernel EntryPoint with real simulation and the reviewed validation policy. Unmodified Alto v1.2.7 fails v0.9 SafeMode decoding; Plether’s pinned patch and a staked Staker passed the local policy suite. The existing live 0.8 Alto service must remain active while an isolated v0.9 canary runs the patched image. Promote only after the separate Nitro-backed service, image, simulator, RPC capability, fee quotes and wallet flows pass. [Alto patch](../../scripts/alto-kernel-patch/README.md) · [Alto service configuration](../../infra/terraform/alto.tf) · [Existing AA rollout constraints](../adr/0002-self-hosted-account-abstraction.md)

## 2. Add self-hosted Kernel execution

Define a versioned manifest for the new deployment. Pin the chain, EntryPoint, Kernel factory, staked Staker, implementation, root validator, paymaster, Perps release and supported actions. Verify counterfactual accounts from the approved Kernel factory and owner initialization data; first UserOperations enter through the Staker. Verify deployed accounts against the pinned implementation and confirm the live root validator and owner at a canonical block. Publish readiness separately for sponsored and ETH-paid operations. [Manifest parser](../../apps/frontend/src/perps-aa/manifest.ts) · [Current account verifier](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/AA/Pimlico.hs#L1297)

In `plether-core`, implement the Kernel-compatible Plether verifying paymaster with pinned EntryPoint and account policy, gas and validity caps, and KMS-backed short-lived authorization. Choose one explicit paymaster envelope and test its hash against the selected EntryPoint, including 0.9’s optional signature encoding. Keep the sponsorship signature bound to the exact operation and policy. Review factory and module trust, how the paymaster identifies execution and nonce modes, and how deployment policy prevents an account implementation change from invalidating the authorization assumptions. Fund and attest the paymaster and start reconciliation from its verified deployment block. Apply the current unsigned-request limits for bounded testing and require owner proof scoped to the intended account and preparation before broader sponsorship. Keep proof credentials independent of transaction signatures. [Paymaster verification and signing](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/AA/Paymaster.hs) · [EntryPoint 0.9 hashing](https://github.com/eth-infinitism/account-abstraction/blob/v0.9.0/contracts/core/UserOperationLib.sol) · [Sponsorship architecture](../adr/0002-self-hosted-account-abstraction.md)

Add a versioned, same-origin Kernel preparation and submission API in the Haskell gateway and route it through the Worker. Share canonical release checks, receipt evidence, quotas, nonce handling and the reviewed inner Perps action rules between gas modes. Decode the selected Kernel outer calls into those inner calls. Validate the account, validator, signature, nonce and execution mode against the pinned profile. Treat unrelated module operations as outside the trading route. Bind preparations to mode, chain, EntryPoint, deployment, sender, nonce and exact calls. Generate the appropriate simulation stub and validate the final signed operation against the stored preparation. [Gateway](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/AA/Gateway.hs) · [Existing preparation](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/AA/Preparation.hs) · [Worker routing](../../apps/frontend/public/_worker.js)

- **Sponsored.** Estimate with the approved paymaster, reserve liability transactionally, sign with KMS, store authorization before returning it, and require that authorization on relay. Maintain account, client and deployment budget limits. Sponsor pause and exhausted quotas affect sponsored issuance. Retain status and recovery for previously issued operations.
- **Pay with ETH.** Require absent paymaster fields during preparation and submission. Estimate and simulate against the actual account, including funded deployment, and check balance and gas bounds. Journal the accepted operation and recovery access before forwarding to Alto. Keep account verification, admission limits and normal bundler validation. This path uses no sponsor reservation, KMS signature, or paymaster deposit. It remains available during sponsor-only outages when account, action and bundler checks pass.

Initialize the new deployment’s PostgreSQL state with versioned preparation, submission and recovery records. Scope operation identity and receipt lookup to the deployment, chain and EntryPoint. Scope authorization, budget and expiry queries to the new paymaster. Reuse the existing reconciliation and circuit breaker logic with the verified Kernel deployment bindings. Keep independent controls for new sponsored issuance, ETH admission and relay health. [Sponsorship storage](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/Database/AaSponsorship.hs) · [Canonical reconciler](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/AA/Reconciler.hs)

## 3. Integrate the frontend and recovery

Add a Kernel adapter at the existing Perps runtime boundary and consume the saved preference before preparing operations. Keep Perps action builders and pricing checks, with separate preparation policies by gas mode. Make account, market, keeper, oracle and bundler readiness distinct from sponsor eligibility so the activity modal stays reachable when sponsorship is unavailable. Expose a general activity opener for links from review and funding states, independent of any operation ID. Use the shared runtime in the trade ticket, account panel, margin and protection confirmations, and activity views. Order tracking and keeper outcomes should remain readable through a sponsorship outage. [Runtime provider](../../apps/frontend/src/perps-aa/PerpsAaProvider.tsx) · [Trading hook](../../apps/frontend/src/hooks/usePerpsTrading.ts) · [Review footer](../../apps/frontend/src/components/PerpsReviewFooter.tsx) · [Activity](../../apps/frontend/src/components/SponsoredOperationActivity.tsx)

Persist attempt version, deployment, chain, sender, EntryPoint, full nonce and nonce key, gas mode, expected paymaster or zero paymaster, reviewed bounds and signed operation hash. Keep a shared trading lock across both modes and tabs for unresolved actions on the same account. Use a server-side fence for competing live submissions where another browser can sign against the same account. Authenticate recovery against the original attempt. Verify canonical EntryPoint events against the saved sender, nonce, hash and paymaster. Store and render the actual cost and distinguish operation inclusion from protocol order commitment and keeper finalization. [Operation journal](../../apps/frontend/src/perps-aa/operationStore.ts) · [Canonical recovery](../../apps/frontend/src/perps-aa/canonicalRecovery.ts)

An unsigned quote can expire in the app. An already signed ETH-paid operation may remain valid after a price quote or Perps order deadline expires and can still consume gas if included. During qualification, prefer an enforceable per-operation account validation deadline that is supported by the chosen stack and respects the order’s submission window. If the release uses an ordinary root signature without this property, ship an owner-reviewed, fee-quoted same-nonce cancellation path proven with Alto. A possible cancellation remains pending until its outcome is canonical. An ambiguous send permits only the same signed operation to be rebroadcast where still appropriate, or explicit cancellation. A timeout, missing receipt or stale quote leaves the operation unresolved. Release the lock based on canonical inclusion, proven nonce displacement, or enforceable expiry with safe-chain evidence. [Existing protocol recovery](../../apps/frontend/src/perps-aa/protocolOperationResolution.ts) · [ERC-4337 nonce and validation rules](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337)

Record payer choice, account profile, funding failures, estimate-to-actual gas, sponsor decisions and recovery outcomes as categorical or aggregated telemetry. Keep wallet credentials and signed operations out of analytics and request logs. [Frontend analytics policy](../../apps/frontend/src/analytics/README.md)

## 4. Onboard new accounts

Derive the Kernel address from the connected owner and the pinned factory, validator initialization data and account index. Use the same derivation for the browser, gateway, funding resolver and registration. Show the verified address before funding and include deployment in the first approved UserOperation. After confirmation, verify that the deployed account has the expected address, implementation and root signer. Exercise first deployment through both gas paths with supported embedded and external wallets. [Funding resolver](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/Handlers/TestnetMarginFunding.hs) · [Registration resolver](../../apps/backend/src/Plether/Handlers/InsightsRegistration.hs)

Where the new deployment uses Sepolia welcome allocations or Insights registration, update those consumers to verify the Kernel address and owner. Keep allocations idempotent by owner and program. Changing the gas setting has no effect on allocation eligibility, account identity or registration.

## 5. Test and roll out

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Contracts and version parity | Exact account and validator artifacts, deterministic address and owner initialization, canonical hashes for both modes, signed gas bounds, and rejected mismatched payment mode, account, paymaster and unsupported action formats. |
| End to end on Arbitrum Sepolia | A collateralized Kernel account with zero native ETH and zero EntryPoint deposit completes an eligible sponsored trade. A Kernel account spends its own ETH through Alto without KMS or sponsor-ledger activity, including first deployment and payment using an existing EntryPoint deposit. Exercise both modes for supported trading actions and test ETH funding and withdrawal, fees, stale quotes and insufficient funds. Verify sponsor outage leaves eligible ETH operations and historical status readable. |
| Preference UX | The switch is accessible in the activity modal without prior transactions and during a sponsorship outage. Switching off changes subsequent transactions to ETH on the same account. Reload and reconnect preserve the setting for that account. Review displays the payer and cost. Cross-tab changes invalidate an unsigned review and preserve the terms of signed operations. |
| Recovery and canary | Verify exactly-once effects through lost responses, retries, refresh and competing tabs, replacement or validated expiry, fee changes, revert and reorg. An operation reviewed in one mode cannot be submitted in the other. Sponsorship liabilities settle against canonical events. Exercise sponsor pause and confirm both-mode activity and withdrawals. |

Extend the Core contract and fork tests, the app’s AA lifecycle and deployment regressions, the Haskell accounting and gateway suites, and the frontend review and activity suites. Cover both gas modes and the persisted modal preference. [AA lifecycle harness](../../scripts/aa-lifecycle-regression.mjs) · [Deployment regression](../../scripts/perps-aa-rollout.test.mjs) · [Accounting tests](../../apps/backend/test/Plether/Database/AaSponsorshipSpec.hs) · [Activity button tests](../../apps/frontend/src/components/__tests__/SponsoredOperationHistoryButton.test.tsx)

Deploy the fresh Kernel stack with sponsorship issuance paused. Attest the account profile, paymaster and RPC bindings before publishing the frontend. Exercise ETH mode with a small test cohort, then enable sponsorship with a canary budget. Launch both choices on Kernel after confirming stable inclusion and recovery, correct actual fee attribution, successful withdrawals, and a working pause control. Track time to inclusion, unresolved attempts, rejected sponsorships, budget use and onboarding failures.

If a rollout fails, pause new account onboarding or the affected submission mode. Keep account access, status, withdrawal and reconciliation paths available wherever the relevant dependencies are healthy. Keep the deployed Kernel bindings for any funded accounts. Drain signed operations and verify canonical outcomes before withdrawing a sponsor deposit or retiring a route.

Treat mainnet as a separate qualification. Production sponsorship must satisfy the existing requirements for account immutability or irreversible upgrade locking, independent RPC evidence, Alto validation, wallet proof and security review. The production account variant must satisfy that policy before setting a launch date. [AA architecture decision](../adr/0002-self-hosted-account-abstraction.md#uups-exception)

## Effort and sequencing

| Workstream | Engineer-days | Exit point |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Version qualification and fork spike | 3–5 | Selected artifacts and first operations in both modes through Alto |
| Core paymaster, Alto and deployment profile | 4–7 | Attested Kernel stack with sponsor funding |
| Gateway, preparation, accounting and recovery | 7–10 | Both payment modes with durable settlement and safe retries |
| Frontend, gas setting and funding UX | 5–8 | Persistent modal toggle, fee review, funding and withdrawals |
| New account onboarding, integration tests and Sepolia canary | 5–8 | Repeatable Kernel onboarding and operator rollout evidence |

Budget **24–38 engineer-days** for the fresh Sepolia release, or roughly **5–8 engineering weeks** for one experienced engineer, plus external review and soak time that cannot overlap. Core, frontend and backend work can proceed in parallel once the version and operation format are pinned. Re-estimate after the fork spike if the selected stack needs a custom validator or a substantially different adapter. Mainnet qualification and follow-on wallet features are outside this estimate.
